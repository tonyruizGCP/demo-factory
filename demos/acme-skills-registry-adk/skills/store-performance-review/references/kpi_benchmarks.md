# ACME Retail Operational KPI Benchmarks (FY2026)

## Corporate Performance Tiers

| KPI Metric | Red (Critical) | Yellow (Needs Attention) | Green (Healthy/Target) | High-Performing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sales Variance vs Budget** | < -5.0% | -5.0% to -0.1% | 0.0% to +5.0% | > +5.0% |
| **Shopper Conversion Rate** | < 20.0% | 20.0% to 24.4% | 24.5% to 28.0% | > 28.0% |
| **Average Order Value (AOV)**| < $55.00 | $55.00 to $69.99 | $70.00 to $85.00 | > $85.00 |
| **Sales Per Labor Hour (SPLH)**| < $160.00 | $160.00 to $184.99 | $185.00 to $220.00 | > $220.00 |
| **Units Per Transaction (UPT)** | < 2.1 | 2.1 to 2.5 | 2.6 to 3.2 | > 3.2 |

## Department Gross Margin Targets
- **Apparel & Outerwear**: Target Margin 48.5%
- **Consumer Electronics**: Target Margin 22.0%
- **Home & Lifestyle**: Target Margin 41.0%
- **Footwear & Accessories**: Target Margin 45.0%

## Labor Guidelines
- When conversion drops below 22.0% while foot traffic is at peak hours, flag as an **Understaffing Friction Event**.
- When SPLH drops below $160.00 while traffic is low, flag as an **Overstaffing Waste Event**.
